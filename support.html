Email Support

Basic calculations

Tap the digits and operators, then =. Multiplication and division come before addition and subtraction: 2 + 3 × 4 = 14. Switch to Scientific to add parentheses: (2 + 3) × 4 = 20.

AC clears the active expression and previous answer. Delete removes the last entry. +/− changes the last operand. Typing a number after a completed result starts a new expression; tapping an operator continues from the result.

Percent follows calculator behavior: 200 + 10% = 220; 200 − 10% = 180; 200 × 10% = 20; 200 ÷ 10% = 2000. A standalone 10% is 0.1.

Scientific functions and angle units

Tap Scientific to reveal the extra keypad. A function opens a parenthesis: tap sin, 3, 0, ), then = for sin(30). In DEG the result is 0.5. In RAD, sin(pi/2) is 1. Check the angle unit before evaluating. Switching DEG/RAD makes the expression ready to recalculate; press = again.

2nd switches sine/cosine/tangent to their inverse functions and changes square/root to cube/cube root. log is base 10; ln is the natural logarithm. π and e are constants. Ans inserts the last successfully calculated answer.

x² and x³ apply to the whole current expression. xʸ raises a value to a power. 1/x takes the reciprocal of the whole expression. Factorial x! accepts integers from 0 through 170. Roots and other functions use real-number arithmetic.

Common errors

You can correct an expression after an error and tap = again. An invalid result does not replace the previous successful answer.

  • Cannot divide by zero: change the denominator to a nonzero value.
  • Missing parenthesis or incomplete expression: close each opened parenthesis and supply a value after the last operator.
  • Square root: use a nonnegative value. Use cube root for a negative real cube root.
  • Logarithms: use a value greater than zero.
  • Inverse sine/cosine: the input must be between −1 and 1.
  • Tangent: some angles, such as 90° and 270°, are undefined.
  • Factorial: use a whole number from 0 to 170.
  • Result too large or undefined: reduce the magnitude or check the expression’s domain.
  • Expression too long: reduce the expression; input is limited to 256 characters.

Rounding and unexpected results

The display shows up to 12 significant digits. Chained calculations keep the internal floating point value, so the displayed rounded number and a manually retyped value may give slightly different later results. Trigonometric results can contain very small floating point residues near zero.

Check parentheses, DEG/RAD, and percentage behavior first. The app does not perform symbolic algebra or arbitrary-precision arithmetic. Verify important calculations independently.

Offline use and calculation history

There is no login, iCloud sync, subscription, or in-app purchase. Each completed calculation is saved to History on your iPhone. Tap the clock icon at the top left of the calculator to view it, tap a calculation to use its result again, or tap Clear to delete all history. History keeps the 100 most recent calculations. The current expression and mode choice are in memory only. A suspended app may keep its current state, but terminating/relaunching or a device restart resets it. We cannot recover a lost expression or deleted history.

Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, and Support are bundled in the app and open offline from the bottom of History. On the welcome screen, Privacy Policy opens the published copy in Safari or your default browser; Terms & Conditions and Support open the published copies in an in-app browser. These need an internet connection. External references and email services need their own network access. The app follows the iPhone’s system light/dark appearance.

Display and keyboard

Swipe a long expression or result horizontally to view it. On a small screen or in landscape, scroll vertically to reach every key and the legal content. Scientific mode can require more scrolling.

The app uses its own keypad, so tapping calculator keys should not open the software keyboard. A connected hardware keyboard can enter numbers, operators, parentheses, and percentages. Enter calculates, Backspace deletes, and Escape or Delete clears. Enter or Space on a focused button activates that button.

If the app does not respond

Tap AC and try a short expression such as 2 + 3. If needed, close and reopen the app; this removes the current calculation. Check for an app or iOS update. Restart the iPhone if the problem persists.

If the calculator shows a restart message, tap Restart calculator. If that fails, reopen it and contact support with the steps that caused the issue. Do not rely on an incorrect result while waiting for help.
