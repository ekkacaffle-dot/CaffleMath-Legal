The app provides Basic and Scientific modes with arithmetic, contextual percentages, real-number functions, parentheses, powers, roots, trigonometry, logarithms, factorial, and a previous-answer value. 3. Accuracy, rounding, and your responsibility

The app uses JavaScript floating point arithmetic and shows results rounded to up to 12 significant digits. Subsequent operations retain the internal numeric value. Floating point rounding, extreme values, invalid inputs, or software errors may affect results. Results are not guaranteed to be exact for every expression.

Standard order of operations applies. Powers associate right-to-left. An immediate percentage in addition or subtraction is relative to the left value: 200 + 10% = 220. In multiplication and division, percentages are fractions: 200 × 10% = 20. Percent is not a remainder operation. Trigonometric results depend on whether DEG or RAD is selected.

You are responsible for checking the expression, parentheses, angle unit, percentage interpretation, and result. Independently verify important calculations with a suitable method. Error messages do not identify every possible mistake or prevent every incorrect result.
