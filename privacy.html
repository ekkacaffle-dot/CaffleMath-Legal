Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Calculations run offline on your iPhone. The calculator does not transmit your expressions or results to us or create an account. Your calculation history is saved only on your iPhone.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Caffle, the developer of Free Cal Maths, provides this policy for the iPhone calculator, optional support correspondence, and these legal/support pages. Contact us at ekka.caffle@gmail.com for questions or privacy requests.

2. Calculations and on-device information

Expressions, results, the previous answer, and mode choices are processed in the running app’s memory and are not sent to a developer server. When you tap =, the expression and result are added to a calculation history saved in the app’s local storage on your iPhone, so it remains after you close and reopen the app. The history keeps your 100 most recent calculations. It is not sent to us, and the app does not sync it to iCloud or any other service. The app also saves a setting recording that you have finished the welcome screen.

The current calculation may remain while the app is suspended. Tap AC to reset the expression and previous answer. Terminating and relaunching the app resets its in-memory calculation state. We cannot restore an expression after that state is lost.

The app reads the system light/dark appearance to display its interface. It does not send that preference to us. The app does not provide iCloud synchronization or a calculation backup/restore service. iOS manages the installed app, cached web assets, device backups, and system snapshots separately; we do not control those operating-system processes.

3. No automatic developer data collection

The calculator has no account registration, advertising, tracking identifiers, analytics SDK, developer-operated crash reporting, location collection, payment processing, or in-app purchases. It does not request camera, microphone, Photos, Contacts, Face ID, location, or tracking access.

We do not sell or share calculator data for advertising, profile users, or use calculations to train an AI model. The calculator and its bundled legal pages work without an internet connection. It does not upload a calculation when you open History or a legal page.

4. Optional support emails

If you choose Email Support, the app opens your email application with our address and a support subject. It does not automatically attach an expression, result, screenshot, identifier, device report, or log, and it does not send the message for you. You decide whether to send it and what to include.

When you send a message, we receive your email address, any name your email service supplies, your message, attachments you choose, and associated email metadata. We use this information to answer you, investigate the issue, handle privacy requests, and follow up when needed. Support is optional and is not required to use the calculator.

Our support mailbox uses Google Gmail. Google and your own email provider process correspondence under their applicable privacy terms. Emails may be processed outside your country. We do not publish your correspondence or use it for advertising. Limited disclosure may be necessary to comply with law, respond to a lawful request, or protect legal rights.

Please use fictional numbers when reporting a calculation issue. Do not send passwords, card numbers, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information. Redact screenshots before sending them.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

We have no server-side calculation history to retain, export, or delete. AC clears the active calculation and previous answer. Clear on the History screen deletes the saved calculation history from your iPhone. Terminating the app clears its in-memory state; uninstalling removes the app and its local container under iOS behavior.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to address and follow up on the request, maintain relevant support records, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation. You can email ekka.caffle@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us. We may ask for proportionate confirmation of the request’s origin to protect another person’s information.

We honor applicable privacy rights, including available rights to restrict or object to processing and withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it. Removing information from our mailbox does not delete copies held by you, another recipient, or a provider under its separate policies. Depending on your location, you may also contact your relevant privacy authority.

6. Website hosting and external links

The copies bundled inside the app, opened from the bottom of History, are local and do not contact a website to display their content. On the welcome screen, the Privacy Policy row opens the published Privacy Policy on GitHub Pages in Safari or your default browser, and the Terms & Conditions and Support rows open the published copies in an in-app browser. Both need an internet connection. If you visit a published copy of these pages on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes hosting traffic such as IP address, browser/device information, and request details under its own privacy statement.

These pages include no developer-added cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, contact forms, remote fonts, or embedded third-party media. Following an external reference or email link is optional and opens a separate service, whose privacy terms apply. Website hosting traffic is separate from your offline calculations.

7. Apple services and diagnostics

Apple handles App Store downloads, Apple account information, and operating-system diagnostics under its own policies. Depending on your iOS sharing settings, Apple may make diagnostic information available to developers. Free Cal Maths does not add a third-party diagnostic uploader or intentionally log your expressions or results. Apple’s system-level services are separate from the calculator’s offline processing.

8. Security and children

We minimize data by keeping calculations and their history on your iPhone and providing no developer calculation server. Keep your iPhone secure and be careful when taking screenshots or sharing support messages. No software, device, or email system can guarantee absolute security.

The calculator does not ask a user’s age or request personal information from children. A parent or guardian should help a child with support correspondence. If you believe a child sent personal information to our support mailbox, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

9. Policy updates and contact

If the app’s information handling changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. Material changes will be described in the app update or another appropriate notice. Where legally required, we will obtain permission before introducing a new processing purpose.

For support and privacy questions, email ekka.caffle@gmail.com.
